FAQ

Is Maplet a private map location saver?

Yes. Maplet requires no accounts and has zero tracking. All your saved places, trip lists, and route plans stay securely on your device and sync via your personal iCloud.

Can I plan multi-stop routes?

Absolutely. Maplet features an advanced multi-stop route planner that can automatically optimize the order of your stops for the fastest driving time, which you can then send directly to Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze.

How do I import existing map locations?

You can batch import locations effortlessly using CSV, GPX, or KML files. You can also export your saved places as a beautiful PDF or raw CSV data.

What is the best alternative to saving places in default map apps?

Maplet is designed for power users who need more than just a giant, cluttered list of starred pins. It acts as a dedicated travel organizer, letting you categorize places into unlimited folders with custom tags, photos, and voice notes.

Can I use Maplet as a delivery route optimizer or sales route planner?

Yes. By adding multiple stops to a single folder, Maplet can automatically optimize the sequence for the most efficient driving route, saving you time and fuel on the road.

How do I share my travel itineraries with friends?

You can easily share entire folders of saved locations and offline deep link or as .maplet files which can be imported by your friends.

Is Maplet good for geocaching and off-grid navigation?

Yes! Maplet is built for the wild. It works completely offline, allowing you to use the Live Compass to track waypoints, Sun, Moon, and your parked car. It also features battery-efficient Dark Mode optimization for long field days.

What coordinate formats does Maplet support?

Maplet's Smart Paste feature instantly detects and converts text coordinates into map pins. We fully support standard Lat/Long, Decimal Degrees, DMS, UTM, and MGRS formats, making it perfect for field professionals.
